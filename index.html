About

Why Product?

I spent 15 years watching the same failure happen in different industries: companies building features their customers didn't need, while the problems customers actually had stayed unsolved.

As a Customer Success and Operations leader, I sat at the intersection of product gaps and customer pain - translating friction into workarounds, escalations into insights, and churn risk into what the product should have been built to do. Increasingly I've applied that instinct one layer up the stack: designing and shipping AI-native products that solve the exact problems I used to escalate.

Now I'm making it official. I bring operational depth that most PMs learn in retrospect: I know what "no resolution" feels like from the customer's side, what "spreadsheet workaround" means for a product team, and why trust - not features - is the real retention driver.

NewZato Labs PM ChallengeB2B SaaS · Agentic AI

Titan - Review Workflow & Agentic AI

Why 60% of review work leaves the platform, and where AI actually helps.

Only 40% of completed work was reviewed inside Reviewer Cockpit; the rest was approved over email and spreadsheets. Notification tickets rose 22% QoQ, split evenly between "too many" and "I missed something important". Leadership wanted agentic AI this year but hadn't decided where.

The plan
5Discovery questions
3AI bets, ranked
2 quartersEvidence-gated roadmap
40% → ↑Reviews completed in-platform
Diagnosis, AI bets & roadmap

Diagnosis

  • Treated all three signals as evidence to interrogate, not problems to fix
  • Cockpit bypass: likely an adoption or narrow-capability gap, not a missing feature
  • Notifications: severity-blindness, not volume
  • Query-blocking time is the one hard, measurable constraint

Where agentic AI helps

  • P1: Pre-submission readiness nudge (upstream fix)
  • P2: Query triage & draft response (ties to the hard gate)
  • P3: Cross-job "what needs you today" Work Queue (Q2 only)

Roadmap logic

Q1: diagnose and prototype the safest bet. Q2: build only what Q1 evidence validates, and if nothing validates, build neither.

Quick fix vs. real fix

Ship a 2-week fix only if it reduces pain without creating debt that blocks the quarter-long fix, and only as a named stopgap.

Enterprise FinTechStabilisation

Payment Platform Stabilisation

Stop the bleed. Restore enterprise trust. One quarter, zero disruption.

Target outcomes
↓35%Sev-1/2 incidents
↓50%Reconciliation mismatches
↓60%Manual recon hours
12 wksDelivery
Problem, approach & solution

The problem

Peak traffic triggered DB lock contention → retry storms → settlement failures. Reconciliation mismatches weren't a reporting bug but a symptom of data written out of order. Ops had become manual middleware.

My approach

  • Telemetry audit by client, payment rail and error code
  • Separated structural errors from timing (sync-lag) errors
  • Ops time-motion study to baseline manual hours

Proposed solutions

  • Idempotent retries with exponential backoff + jitter
  • 30-min reconciliation buffer after peak windows
  • Exception triage dashboard (Retool) to get Ops out of Excel

Key insight

Operational stabilisation over platform transformation: restore predictability for enterprise clients within a single quarter.

Swiggy AI - Rider Support Resolution

From raising tickets to resolving issues within the same interaction.

Target outcomes
35%→60%+Automation rate
↓Repeat queries
↑Post-interaction CSAT
Problem, diagnosis & solution

The problem

Riders with payout questions got generic AI responses and no resolution. Four gaps - Explanation, Context, Action, Ownership - each eroded trust at a different stage.

Core diagnosis

The AI was built for queries, not situations: keyword matching with no decision layer. Wrong financial explanations destroyed trust fastest.

Context-aware resolution system

  • Uses order, earnings and incentive context to explain "why"
  • AI resolves above 90% confidence; humans take the rest
  • P0 focus: earnings clarity

Rollout

Pilot in one high-volume city (Hindi + English + local language) → 3–5 cities → pan-India with vernacular voice and proactive nudges.
